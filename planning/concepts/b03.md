# Batch b03 concepts (131 protocols; mostly multi-omic / single-cell / proximity / perturbation / RNA-modification / 10x chemistries)

Legend: [U] ubiquitous (>~25 protocols in batch), [C] common (8-25), [R] rare/specialised (<8). Counts are rough. Triage: full notes read for ~90; rest judged from catalogue one-liners (10x variants, RNA-mod methods, TRAPS-seq, TF-chRDP, UDA-seq, wellda-seq, TRIP, scG2P etc.).

## 1. Enzymes: activities and specificities
- [U] Reverse transcriptases (MMLV-type SuperScript II/III/IV, Maxima H-, SMARTScribe): RNA-dependent DNA pol, RNase H-, terminal transferase / template-switch activity; in-nucleus RT; temperature/betaine/PEG/MgCl2 effects. SHARE-seq, Smart-seq-based G&T-seq, 10x.
- [R] Alternative RTs: AMV (dNTP-limited RT stops, 2OMe-seq), TGIRT group-II intron RT (ac4C-seq), Bst 3.0 as RT/strand-displacing pol (ChAR-seq, MUSIC).
- [U] Thermostable proofreading/hot-start PCR polymerases (Q5, KAPA HiFi, Phusion, SeqAmp, Deep Vent exo-): fidelity, GC bias, cycles. All.
- [C] Klenow exo-/Klenow fragment (dA-tailing, fill-in, second-strand): iMARGI, MUSIC, scCOOL-seq, scNMT-seq, 3'T-fill.
- [C] Tn5 transposase: transposition + fragmentation + adapter insertion in one step; loaded transposomes (A/A, B/B, A/B, one-adaptor Tn5, pA/pG-Tn5), 9-bp duplication, SDS/EDTA stop, Tn5 gap fill (72 C). ISSAAC-seq, SHARE-seq, scONE-seq, Paired-seq, TaDRIM-seq.
- [C] Tn5 acting on RNA/cDNA hybrids (tagmenting cDNA:mRNA) and Nextera XT/TDE1 library kits. SUM-seq, SNARE-seq, ISSAAC-seq.
- [U] T4 DNA ligase (sticky/blunt, nick sealing, splint ligation, proximity ligation; PEG, ATP, temperature/time). SHARE-seq, scCARE-seq, GAGE-seq, QBC.
- [C] RNA ligases: T4 RNA ligase 1 (ssRNA, RNA-linker), T4 RNA ligase 2 / truncated KQ (pre-adenylated donor, ATP-free, nick/splint-RNA), RnlII. ChAR-seq, GRID-seq, MUSIC, 3P-seq, aa-tRNA-seq.
- [C] Restriction enzymes (4-cutters DpnII/MboI/AluI/MspI/NlaIII/HpyCH4V; type IIS FokI/SapI/MmeI; rare cutters NotI/SbfI/PacI) as fragmentation, barcode-removal or modality-selective cleavage tools; sticky-end compatibility; methylation sensitivity. sn-m3C-seq, Paired-seq, GRID-seq, Stapleton.
- [R] DpnI (cuts only GATC-6mA) vs DpnII (blocked by 6mA): Dam methylation readout. scDam&T-seq, EpiDamID.
- [R] Methyltransferases as footprinting/marking: Dam (6mA at GATC), M.CviPI (GpC 5mC; accessibility), SAM cofactor. scNMT-seq, NOMe-HiC, scNOMe-seq, EpiDamID, scDam&T-seq.
- [C] Polynucleotide kinase T4 PNK: 5'-phosphorylation and 3'-phosphate removal (3'-phosphatase activity) to make ends ligatable. GRID-seq, MUSIC, TaDRIM-seq.
- [C] Phosphatases (CIP, rSAP, FastAP, Antarctic): remove 5'/3' phosphates to block self-ligation or prepare end chemistry. AlkAniline-seq, ac4C-seq, 5PSeq.
- [R] Terminal transferase TdT (poly-C/dA/dG tailing) as adapter-free second-strand anchor. Paired-seq, PairedTag, GAGE-seq.
- [C] E. coli poly(A) polymerase (RNA tailing for dT priming). MUSIC, ChAR-seq-like.
- [C] Exonucleases: Exo I (remove excess primers), thermolabile Exo I, lambda exo, T5 exo (circle enrichment), Exo III. SUM-seq, scCARE-seq, Stapleton.
- [C] DNA polymerases for gap fill / strand displacement: Phi29 (MDA, RCA), Bst large fragment, DNA Pol I + RNase H (nick-translation second strand), T4 DNA pol. G&T-seq, DR-seq, ChAR-seq, scRICA-seq, PNA.
- [R] Cas9 RNP as sequence-specific nuclease for depletion (DASH); cutting 3 bp upstream of PAM; library molecules lose adapter site. DASH.
- [R] RNases P/Z (tRNA processing for multi-guide arrays), RNase H (hybrid cleavage; release tail-shortened RNA), RNase T1 (G-specific), RNase If. CROPseq-multi, 3P-seq, TaDRIM-seq.
- [R] UDG/USER (uracil removal) for strand-specific/primer-removal chemistry and abasic cleavage. snmC2T-seq, ChAR-seq, sccomplete-seq.
- [R] Photocatalysts/enzymes for proximity labeling: APEX2 (biotin-phenol + H2O2), tyramide/photocatalytic (PhoTag). APEX-seq, APEX-RIP, PhoTag.
- [R] Recorder enzymes: Cas9 + indel scars, prime editors (pegRNA), RT-Cas1/Cas2 spacer acquisition, piggyBac transposase (calling cards). DNA Typewriter, LINNAEUS, Record-seq, scCC.
- [R] T7 RNA polymerase for IVT, RNA ligase on pre-adenylated adapters, Dam/DamID adaptors; HEI/imidazolide chemistry for aminoacyl-tRNA. see 5.

## 2. Nucleic-acid chemistry and base conversions
- [C] Bisulfite conversion (C -> U -> T; 5mC/5hmC protected); strand loss/degradation, post- vs pre-bisulfite adapter tagging (PBAT), converted strands carry U. scM&T-seq, scNMT-seq, sn-m3C-seq, snmC2T-seq, scCOOL-seq.
- [R] Reduced-representation (MspI/RRBS) with methylated adapters; premethylated adapters survive bisulfite. scMT-seq, scTrio-seq.
- [R] Non-CG/CpG context separation computationally; 5-methyl-dCTP incorporation to distinguish synthesized DNA from original. snmC2T-seq.
- [R] GpC vs CpG methylation (accessibility marking at GpC; endogenous CpG). NOMe-HiC, scNOMe-seq.
- [R] Targeted bisulfite amplicons (primers for converted sequence). scTEM-seq.
- [R] Chemical RNA-modification mapping: NaCNBH3 reduction (ac4C), alkaline hydrolysis + aniline cleavage at abasic sites (m7G/m3C), generating new 5'-phosphate. ac4C-seq, AlkAniline-seq.
- [R] RT-stop / misincorporation readout; dNTP-limited RT to stop at 2'-O-methyl (2OMe-seq).
- [R] Metabolic/chemical labels: AHA (azidohomoalanine) click capture of nascent peptide-ribosome-RNA; biotin-phenol adducts. AHARIBO, APEX-seq.
- [R] Aminoacyl-tRNA chemistry: imidazolide-activated adapter, N-P phosphoramidate bridge to charged tRNA amino acid. aa-tRNA-seq.
- [C] Click chemistry for antibody-oligo conjugation: DBCO/azide (SPAAC), TCO/tetrazine (IEDDA), HyNic/4FB (Solulink), NHS-amine, maleimide-thiol; excess-oligo removal (FPLC, MWCO, ammonium sulfate). Phospho-seq, DAb-seq, LIBRA-seq, ClickTags, SCITO-seq.
- [C] Reversible crosslinking: formaldehyde (fixation, reversal with SDS/proteinase K/65-70 C), DSG/DSP/SPDP (cleavable disulfide; DTT reversal), glycine quench. DOGMA-seq, QuRIE-seq, RAID, Spatial Hi-C-RNA, ChAR-seq.
- [R] Photocleavable (UV) linkers and photocaged oligos for release. GeoMx DSP, Slide-tags, ZipSeq, DAb-seq (UV-released primers).
- [R] Hydrogels / acrydite 5'-modified oligos to anchor nucleic acids into polymer matrices. Hybrid BAG-seq, FIND-seq, Gel-seq.
- [R] Lipid/cholesterol-modified oligos anchoring to cell membranes (hashing). MULTI-seq, ZipSeq.

## 3. Oligo / construct design features
- [U] Illumina P5/P7, Read 1 / Read 2 (TruSeq vs Nextera/ME) handles, i5/i7 indexes, partial adapters completed by indexing PCR; hybrid TruSeq/Nextera constructs needing mixed sequencing primers. SHARE-seq, ISSAAC-seq, GAGE-seq.
- [U] Cell barcodes (whitelists; bead, well, split-pool, ligation-derived) and UMIs (random N windows, collapsing PCR duplicates); UMI vs random-priming coordinate vs sample index vs reporter tag. DR-seq, 5PSeq, 10x.
- [C] Antibody-derived tags (ADT/HTO/TotalSeq-A/B/C): 5' amine/azide oligo with Read 2 handle + barcode + poly(A) (A) or capture sequence (B/C); hashtag oligos; additive primers to co-amplify tags. CITE-seq, ECCITE-seq, REAP-seq, inCITE-seq, MULTI-seq.
- [C] Template-switch oligo (TSO) with rGrG+G (LNA) and its SMART handle; 5' vs 3' libraries; ACAT vs no-linker variants. G&T-seq, scCAT-seq, FIND-seq, Paired-Damage-seq.
- [C] Poly(dT)-VN primers with handle + barcode + UMI; anchored V/N; poly(A)-tailed tags capturing on dT; biotinylated RT primers (internal biotin-dT) for later streptavidin pulldown. sci-CAR-seq, scCARE-seq, ISSAAC-seq.
- [C] Capture-sequence feature barcoding (CS1/CS2 extended, 5' direct capture, spike-in RT primers): guides, antibodies. 5' direct-capture Perturb-seq, Direct-seq, scSNV-seq, SPEAR-ATAC, CROPseq-multi.
- [C] Splints / bridge / linker oligos for split-pool barcode ligation, plus blocking/terminator oligos (reverse complements to sequester unreacted arms); phasing/stagger N bases. SHARE-seq, Paired-seq, PairedTag, QBC, GAGE-seq.
- [C] Blocked ends: 3' inverted-dT, 3' ddC, C3 spacer, 3' amine/phosphate; 5' phosphate for ligation; phosphorothioate (PTO) bonds protecting from exonuclease/proofreading degradation; LNA; pre-adenylated 5'-App adapters. pMENTs, SUM-seq, GRID-seq, 3P-seq.
- [C] Biotin modifications (5', internal biotin-dT, desthiobiotin, 3' biotin) for streptavidin capture / separation. ChAR-seq, G&T-seq, GoT-ChA.
- [R] Bivalent RNA-DNA bridge linkers (RNA-compatible end + DNA sticky end, internal biotin, PacI/DpnII/AluI site) joining RNA to proximal DNA; orientation from linker; RNA/DNA chimeras. ChAR-seq, GRID-seq, iMARGI, MUSIC, TaDRIM-seq.
- [R] Padlock/proximity probes, PID-UMI padlocks, adjacent probe pairs (PLA-style, Flex, PLAYR, PrimeFlow), proximity extension (PEA). Proximity Network Assay, Olink Explore, Prox-seq, 10x Flex.
- [R] Random-barcode reporter libraries (MPRA/STARR-seq tags, TRIP 16-nt barcodes, iBAR, transcribed PuroR barcode) linking sequence/guide/insertion to expression. MPRA, STARR-seq, TRIP, scSNV-seq.
- [R] Lineage/recorder barcodes (CRISPR scar arrays, DNA Tape, spacer acquisition). LINNAEUS, scGESTALT, ScarTrace, DNA Typewriter, Record-seq.
- [R] Guide RNA design features: scaffold poly(A)/capture tail (8A8G), tRNA-flanked guide arrays, internal barcodes (iBAR) in scaffold loop, U6 Pol III cassettes in lentiviral LTR (CROP-seq duplication). Direct-seq, CROPseq-multi.
- [R] RNA reporter tags (capped synthetic polyA RNA with UMI + antibody ID). RAID.
- [R] Antigen/peptide DNA barcodes on streptavidin/pMHC multimers (BCR/TCR specificity). LIBRA-seq, TetTCR-seq.
- [R] Lectin- or nanobody/phage-displayed tags (PAC peptide captured by scATAC bead). SUGAR-seq, scGR-seq, PHAGE-ATAC.
- [R] Spatial barcodes: Slide-tags bead puck oligos, DBiT A/B channels, Visium spot barcode (16 nt + 12 nt UMI), Seq-Scope arrays, GeoMx photocleavable probe tags. 
- [R] Alu/LINE-1 repeat primer panels; targeted amplicon panels with CS overhangs (Tapestri). scTEM-seq, SDR-seq, scG2P.

## 4. Fragmentation
- [U] Tn5 tagmentation (see 1); [C] mechanical (Covaris acoustic shearing to ~200-500 bp); [C] restriction-enzyme fragmentation; [R] RNA fragmentation (Mg2+ heat, alkaline hydrolysis); [R] chemical/random-primer fragmentation via second-strand random priming; [R] Cas9 cutting (DASH).
- [R] Random-priming from 3' tags (random hexamers carrying handles) instead of fragmenting. EpiDamID, scGESTALT, G&T-seq.

## 5. End processing and adapter addition
- [U] End repair, A-tailing (dA) and TA-ligation of Y/forked adapters; blunt vs sticky; fill-in of 5' overhangs (biotin-dATP fill-in in Hi-C; no fill-in for GATC-compatible ligation). NOMe-HiC, iMARGI, Stapleton, Spatial Hi-C-RNA.
- [C] Gap fill after tagmentation (72 C); partial adapter + indexing PCR completing P5/P7.
- [C] Template switching (5' add-on), polyG/polyC tails, TdT tailing.
- [R] Circularization (intramolecular ligation, NEBuilder/Gibson, dilute T4 ligase, T5 exo enrichment) to move barcodes next to targets or to link ends. scCC, scRICA-seq, Stapleton long reads.
- [R] Strand-specific selection by chemistry: tail-copy + RNase H release (3P-seq), 3' end fill (3'T-fill on-cluster unlabeled fill), 5'-phosphate-dependent ligation to native 5'P (5PSeq, 3'READS poly(A) capture).
- [R] Adaptase / post-bisulfite single-strand adapter addition; random priming with tailed primers (PBAT-like). snmC2T-seq, sn-m3C-seq.
- [R] Poly(A) selection by oligo(dT)/biotin-dT (T45 DNA/RNA bridge) and 3' end capture with RNA 3' adapters. 3P-seq, 3'READS.

## 6. Ligation types
- [C] Split-pool barcode ligation (hybridization + ligation, 2-4 rounds, 96/384 wells; blocker added; one final ligase to seal nicks). SHARE-seq, Paired-seq, GAGE-seq, QBC, UDA-seq.
- [C] Splint-directed (bridged) DNA-DNA ligation; RNA-templated probe ligation (RNA-splinted, SplintR-like in Flex/PLAYR).
- [C] Proximity ligation (Hi-C style) in situ in nuclei, DpnII/MboI/DdeI/MseI digest; RNA-DNA proximity via bridge. HiRES, sn-m3C-seq, Spatial Hi-C-RNA.
- [C] RNA 3' ligation of pre-adenylated adapters (truncated ligase, ATP-free) / RNA 5' ligation (5PSeq, ac4C-seq).
- [R] Adjacent-probe ligation (RTL) in GEMs for fixed RNA; gap-fill-then-ligate padlocks (PNA).
- [R] Microfluidic (DBiT) perpendicular-channel ligation giving x-y barcode.
- [R] Cohesive end (type IIS / PacI) cloning of designed oligo libraries (MPRA, STARR-seq, CROPseq).

## 7. Amplification modes
- [U] PCR with limited cycles; qPCR cycle-number determination; exponential vs linear (one-primer) amplification (CRISPR-sciATAC, GoT-ChA, SUM-seq linear barcoding cycles).
- [C] Nested/hemi-nested PCR for rare targets (guide, scar, VDJ, GoT-ChA, AIRR 5'RACE, LIBRA-seq).
- [C] Multiplex targeted PCR in droplets (Tapestri) combining gDNA and cDNA amplicons, UV-released barcode primers. DAb-seq, SDR-seq, scG2P, TARGET-seq.
- [C] Pre-amplification of tagged material before splitting for multiple libraries. SHARE-seq, DOGMA-seq, Paired-seq.
- [R] T7 in vitro transcription (linear amplification; inDrops/CEL-seq-style, 15 h IVT). QuRIE-seq, scGESTALT, scDam&T-seq, DR-seq.
- [R] Rolling-circle amplification (padlock gap-fill, PLAYR circle, short in-situ RCA for PNA), Phi29 on circles (scRICA-seq).
- [R] MDA / PicoPLEX / quasilinear WGA (random priming cycles with Bst) for gDNA. G&T-seq, DR-seq, Hybrid BAG-seq.
- [R] Branched-DNA (bDNA) signal amplification (preamplifier/amplifier/label probe) with no nucleic-acid amplification. PrimeFlow.
- [R] Inverse PCR for mapping integration sites. TRIP.
- [R] Random-priming PCR with stepped annealing (4 -> 65 C); on-bead PCR. HiRES, ChAR-seq.
- [R] Proximity extension (PEA) amplicons. Olink Explore.

## 8. Capture, enrichment, depletion
- [C] Streptavidin bead capture (M-280, MyOne C1/T1) to separate biotinylated cDNA/linker junctions from non-biotin DNA; supernatant vs beads fractions. SHARE-seq, GoT-ChA, spatial-ATAC-RNA-seq.
- [C] Oligo(dT) bead/biotin-dT physical separation of mRNA vs gDNA from one lysate (G&T-seq, scM&T-seq, scNMT-seq, Gel-seq).
- [C] SPRI size-fraction partition: 0.6x supernatant (short ADT/HTO) vs beads (long cDNA); both kept. CITE-seq family, MULTI-seq, SPOTS, QuRIE-seq.
- [R] Hybrid capture with biotinylated probes for deep mtDNA/variants. ReDeeM, MAESTER-like.
- [R] Antibody-based capture: ChIP-like targeting via pA/pG-Tn5 (CUT&Tag), antibody+Dam-ProteinA (EpiDamID), CoTECH, PairedTag, TaDRIM-seq, Spatial CUT&Tag-RNA.
- [R] Affinity selection of proximity-labeled RNA (streptavidin after APEX/biotin-phenol); nascent-peptide/ribosome-associated RNA capture. APEX-seq, APEX-RIP, AHARIBO.
- [R] Cas9 depletion (DASH); rRNA depletion (RiboZero, Flex probe hybridization), poly(A) selection vs total RNA.
- [R] FACS sorting of live/fixed cells into wells; index sorting; TaqMan droplet sorting to enrich rare cells. TARGET-seq, FIND-seq, HiRES.
- [R] Target enrichment by specific RT primers/panels; mRNA target-specific RT. TARGET-seq, SDR-seq, scSNV-seq.
- [R] Nanovial/secretion capture. SEC-seq, TRAPS-seq.

## 9. Cleanup, size selection, QC
- [U] SPRI/AMPure bead ratio cleanup and double-sided size selection; silane (Dynabeads MyOne) cleanup; column cleanup (Zymo, Qiagen). All.
- [C] Gel/PAGE size selection; [C] TapeStation/Bioanalyzer/qPCR library QC; cycle-calibration qPCR; [R] spike-ins (ERCC, lambda DNA, IVT controls) for QC/normalization; [R] on-bead enzymatic steps with washes.
- [R] Carrier ssDNA/tRNA for low-input recovery. scCARE-seq, QBC.
- [R] Primer dimer / primer-removal (Exo I, UDG) and additive primers.

## 10. Compartmentalization and combinatorial indexing
- [C] Droplet (10x Chromium gel beads, inDrops, Drop-seq, Tapestri): bead barcode + UMI, cell lysis in GEM, emulsion break, recovery agent; Poisson loading; overloading and unpacking. 10x family, OAK, SUM-seq.
- [C] Plate/well-based single-cell (FACS into 96/384 wells; per-well RT primers): Smart-seq2/3, CEL-seq2, scM&T-seq, sci-CAR-seq.
- [C] Split-and-pool combinatorial indexing (2-4 rounds), nuclei as compartments: SHARE-seq, Paired-seq, GAGE-seq, QBC.
- [R] Hashing / sample multiplexing with barcoded antibodies, lipids, lectins, Tn5, or RT primers, enabling droplet overloading and super-loading. MULTI-seq, SUM-seq, scITO-seq, ClickTags.
- [R] Hydrogel-bead/gel-based compartments (BAG-seq, Gel-seq), nanovials, microwells.
- [R] Physical nucleus/cytoplasm separation by lysis & centrifugation (scMT-seq, scTrio-seq, scCAT-seq, LiMCA).
- [R] Two-stage encapsulation (RT in first emulsion; barcoding in second). scG2P.

## 11. Multi-omics co-assay strategies (joint readouts)
- [C] Same-molecule vs separate-molecule readouts; physical separation of RNA and DNA (beads/lysis) vs co-barcoding the same cell with shared identity. G&T-seq, scNMT-seq, sci-CAR-seq.
- [C] RNA + ATAC: in-situ Tn5 then RT, shared barcodes (SHARE-seq, SNARE-seq, ISSAAC-seq, 10x Multiome/DOGMA/TEA-seq/ICICLE-seq, SUM-seq, easySHARE-seq).
- [C] RNA + protein (CITE-seq, REAP-seq, ECCITE-seq, inCITE-seq, QuRIE-seq, DBiT-seq, SPOTS).
- [R] RNA + histone marks (PairedTag, CoTECH, Spatial CUT&Tag-RNA), RNA + 3D genome (HiRES, GAGE-seq, sciCARE, LiMCA, MUSIC), DNA methylation (scM&T, scNMT, snmC2T), genotype (TARGET-seq, GoT-ChA, SDR-seq), CRISPR guides (Perturb-seq variants, CRISPR-sciATAC, SPEAR-ATAC), TCR/BCR + antigen (LIBRA-seq, TetTCR-seq, 5' VDJ).
- [R] RNA-chromatin and RNA-RNA proximity (ChAR-seq, GRID-seq, iMARGI, MUSIC, TaDRIM-seq, Spatial Hi-C-RNA, PRIM-seq).
- [R] Lineage-recording + transcriptome (LINNAEUS, scGESTALT, ScarTrace, DNA Typewriter); mitochondrial mutations for lineage (ReDeeM).

## 12. Spatial
- [R] Array-capture (Visium, Seq-Scope): permeabilization, on-slide RT, second strand, KOH elution.
- [R] Barcode-transfer: Slide-tags (photocleaved bead oligos into nuclei), ZipSeq (photocaged tags), DBiT-seq (perpendicular microfluidic ligation A x B), spatial-ATAC-RNA-seq.
- [R] Region-of-interest UV release (GeoMx DSP), SPOTS (Visium + antibodies).
- [R] Tissue fixation/permeabilization (formaldehyde, digitonin/NP-40/saponin, proteinase K/SDS), reverse crosslinking.

## 13. Proximity / crosslinking
- [R] Hi-C chemistry (formaldehyde/DSG, restriction, fill-in or biotin fill-in, in-situ proximity ligation, crosslink reversal). HiRES, NOMe-HiC, sn-m3C-seq.
- [R] RNA-chromatin crosslink + bridge ligation (GRID-seq, ChAR-seq); RNA-RNA proximity (TaDRIM-seq).
- [R] Protein proximity: PLA, proximity extension, padlock edge reporters (Prox-seq, PNA, Olink Explore).
- [R] Enzymatic proximity labeling (APEX, photocatalytic). 

## 14. Perturbation / reporter assays
- [R] Pooled CRISPR screens with scRNA/ATAC readout; guide capture (direct, CS handles, CROP-seq), cassette duplication in LTR.
- [R] Massively parallel reporter assays (barcode-in-transcript), self-transcribing STARR-seq (enhancer in 3'UTR), TRIP integration-site mapping.
- [R] Calling cards (piggyBac self-reporting transposon). scCC.

## 15. Sequencer-specific read structure and custom primers
- [C] Illumina paired-end with asymmetric read lengths for barcode+UMI on R1 (e.g. 28+90 for 10x v2/v3) vs barcode on both; index reads (i5/i7, dual unique indexes); custom Read 1/Read 2/index primers for non-standard constructs (CROPseq-multi, SPEAR-ATAC, scCC, SNARE-seq).
- [C] Read structure computed from final construct (which primer lands where); phasing/stagger and low-diversity libraries (PhiX, N0-3 stagger). Paired-seq, SDR-seq.
- [R] Single-end vs paired-end for barcoded recorders; PacBio long-read isoform/lineage routes (DNA Typewriter, scRICA-seq); Nanopore direct tRNA (aa-tRNA-seq); MGI/BGI DNB conversion (scCAT-seq, scONE-seq).
- [R] On-cluster fill (3'T-fill) and on-flowcell chemistry specific tricks.
- [R] Combining libraries of differing modalities in a single run (pooling ratios e.g. 1:9; modality-specific i5/i7 indexes).

## 16. 10x Genomics commercial chemistries (reference platform)
- [C] 3' GE v1-v4 and GEM-X (gel-bead oligo: Read1 + 16 nt BC + 10-12 nt UMI + dT30VN; RT in GEM, template switch, cDNA PCR, fragmentation/end repair/A-tailing/ligation/index PCR); 5' GE and V(D)J (TSO on bead, TCR/BCR enrichment with nested constant-region primers); Feature Barcode (antibody, CRISPR); Flex fixed-RNA probes; Visium; Multiome/scATAC (nuclei, transposition, bead BC + linear barcoding PCR).
- [R] Dual-index kits, Dynabeads cleanup, SPRIselect ratios; ATAC GEM linear PCR; CS1/CS2 guide scaffold.

## 17. Other concepts worth teaching (appear across protocols)
- [C] Strand/orientation logic: sense vs antisense, TSO vs RT primer ends, RNA vs DNA fate of same molecule; RNA template persistence in RNA:DNA hybrids.
- [C] Primer design: anchoring, Tm, hybrid TruSeq/Nextera arms, terminal PTO, additive "bridging" primers, spike-in RT primers.
- [C] Barcode design theory: whitelists, Hamming distance, collisions/doublets, barcode swapping, index hopping.
- [R] Bioinformatic read collapse (UMI/random barcodes), doublet filtering as non-chemical "removal".
- [R] Controls: positive/negative (mock, alkali), spike-ins, NTC.
- [R] Stoichiometry/dilution effects: split fractions (e.g. 25/35/35%), carryover of unremoved reagents, co-present catalysts.
