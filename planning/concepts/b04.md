## 1. Nucleic-acid chemistry fundamentals
- RNA 5' end states: 5'-triphosphate (primary transcript), 5'-monophosphate (processed), 5'-OH, 5' cap (m7G, 5'-5' triphosphate), NAD cap [C] - dRNA-seq, Cappable-seq, CAGE, NAD captureSeq, CapZyme-Seq
- RNA 3' end states: 3'-OH, 2',3'-cyclic phosphate, 3'-phosphate, 3'-aminoacyl (tRNA), 2'-O-methyl-blocked 3' end [C] - cP-RNA-seq, cyPhyRNA-seq, MSR-seq, Nm-seq, HAC-seq
- Ligation compatibility requires 5'P/3'OH pairs; hence kinase/phosphatase end repair [U]
- Strand polarity/directionality: which strand is sequenced; read orientation vs RNA sense [U] - NEBNext Ultra II Directional, dUTP methods
- RNA vs DNA templates: RNA-RNA vs DNA-RNA hybrids, RNA lability (alkaline hydrolysis, divalent-cation fragmentation) [U]
- RNA secondary structure / G-quadruplex / duplexes as determinants of enzymatic accessibility [C] - G4RP-seq, hiCLIP, icSHAPE
- Modified nucleotides as RT-obstacles: m1A, m3C, m1G, m6A, m7G, m5C, Nm (2'-O-Me), Psi, tRNA modifications [C] - ARM-seq, m1A-seq, Nm-seq, HydraPsiSeq
- Circular RNA / junction-spanning reads (backsplice) [R] - circTAIL-seq, FISSEQ

## 2. Enzymes: activities and specificities
- Reverse transcriptases (MMLV/SuperScript II/III/IV, Maxima H-, SSIV VILO): RNase H activity (-), processivity, strand displacement, terminal transferase / non-templated C addition [U]
- TGIRT group-II intron RT: high processivity, structured/modified RNA read-through, template switching from RNA/DNA duplex without TSO [R] - DM-tRNA-seq, Butt-seq, mim-tRNAseq, DMS-MaPseq, Nano-tRNAseq
- RT fidelity modulation: Mn2+ buffers promote misincorporation/read-through at modified bases (MaP) [R] - DMS-MaPseq, M3-seq, m7G-MaP-seq
- RT-stop vs misincorporation vs deletion signatures at modified/adducted bases [C] - miCLIP, iCLIP, m1A-ID-seq, Nm-Mut-seq
- RNA ligases: T4 RNA ligase 1 (ssRNA, ATP, 5'P/3'OH), T4 Rnl2 truncated KQ/K227Q (adenylated donor, no self-circularization), Rnl2 on RNA-templated nicks (DNA splints) [U] - eCLIP, iCLIP, NEBNext small RNA, LISH-seq
- T4 DNA ligase (dsDNA/ splint-assisted) and ssDNA ligase CircLigase [C] - cDNA circularization, EasySci, HDST
- Pre-adenylated (5'App) adapters: ligation without ATP, prevents adapter concatemers [C] - iCLIP, D-seq, easyCLIP
- T4 PNK: 5' phosphorylation (ATP), 3' phosphatase (3'-OH generation), 32P labelling [C] - iCLIP, eCLIP, FLASH, miCLIP
- Phosphatases (CIP/rSAP/CAP-CIP), Tobacco Acid Pyrophosphatase (TAP) / RppH / Cap-Clip: cap/PPP -> 5'P conversion [C] - dRNA-seq, Cappable-seq, CAGE
- Terminator 5'-phosphate-dependent exonuclease (TEX): degrades processed 5'P RNA, spares 5'PPP [R] - dRNA-seq
- RNases: RNase A/T1 (CLIP trimming), RNase H (RNA of hybrid; rRNA depletion; probe removal), RNase R (linear RNA degradation, circRNA enrichment), RNase I, RNase A for cDNA protection (single-strand digest) [C] - eCLIP, M3-seq, LIGR-seq, DRAP3R
- DNases (DNase I), Proteinase K (crosslink reversal, protein digestion) [U]
- Poly(A) polymerase (3' tailing, cap capture) [C] - CAGE, Cappable-seq, FLAM-seq
- Terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase (dC/dG tailing) [R] - MATQ-seq, FLAM-seq, ClickSeq
- Vaccinia capping enzyme (VCE) with GTP analogue (3'-desthiobiotin-GTP): labels 5'PPP RNA [R] - Cappable-seq
- E. coli poly(A) polymerase, T4 PNK 3'-phosphatase-only (ATP-free PNK) [R] - Cappable-seq
- Exonuclease I (remove excess primer; ssDNA 3'-5') [C] - m6A-ORL-Seq, MAESTER
- DNA polymerases: Q5/KAPA/Phusion high-fidelity, Taq-based, Klenow/Bst; strand displacement; polymerase read-through of dU (KAPA HiFi tolerates, Phusion does not) [U]
- Uracil handling: UNG, USER (UDG + endoVIII) to degrade dU-marked second strand [C] - NEBNext Ultra II Directional, DRIPc-seq
- Restriction enzymes (BamHI, BsaI, BstI, MmeI-like tag release) used for tag trimming/library structuring [R] - CAGE, miCLIP NSUN2, Click display
- Cas9 digestion for depletion of abundant/unwanted cDNA [R] - MAD-DASH
- RNase-modification-sensitive nucleases: MazF (cleaves ACA unless m6A methylated), Nuclease P1 (ssRNA), RNase T1/A, eEndoV (inosine), P1 [R] - MAZTER-seq, FragSeq, EndoVIPER-seq
- Mod-writing / mod-removing enzymes: AlkB/D135S (dealkylation), FTO, NSUN2 C271A trap, APOBEC1-YTH fusion (C-to-U), m6A methyltransferase analogues [R] - ARM-seq, DM-tRNA-seq, DART-seq, m6A-SEAL, miCLIP NSUN2
- T7 RNA polymerase: promoter-dependent IVT, linear amplification, BrUTP incorporation; NASBA (T7 + RT + RNase H) [C] - CEL-Seq, inDrop, MARS-seq, LAST-seq, INSIGHT
- RNA polymerase run-on (nuclear run-on, Pol II elongating complex retains RNA) [R] - GRO-seq, ChRO-seq, fastGRO, GRO-cap
- RNA polymerase with NTP analogues / CapZyme (cap-dependent initiation tag) [R] - CapZyme-Seq
- Reverse transcription w/ DNA hybrid-capture antibodies (S9.6) [R] - DRIPc-seq
- LAMP polymerase (Bst) with 6 primers, strand-displacing isothermal amplification [R] - LAMP-Seq, LamPORE
- Cell-free translation / puromycin fusion for display selections [R] - CFMS mRNA display, Click display
- Tn5 transposase (tagmentation) incl. loaded with mosaic-end oligos; RNA/DNA hybrid tagmentation [C] - Drop-seq, Microwell-seq, FIPRESCI, Light-Seq
