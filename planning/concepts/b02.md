## 1. Enzymes and their activities/specificities
- Type II restriction enzymes, blunt vs sticky, 4-cutter vs 6/8-cutter, methylation sensitivity [C]: MspI/HpaII (CCGG, CpG-rich "reduced representation", methylation-sensitive), SbfI/EcoRI (RAD), NlaIII/DpnII/MboI/HindIII/AluI/HpyCH4V/CviKI-1 (RELACS, scSPRITE, Hi-C family, scEdU-seq). e.g. RRBS (Illumina), RAD-seq, Rapture, scRRBS, Stevens single-cell Hi-C
- Type IIS / Class-2 restriction-based "restriction-site-adapter" background suppression [R]: Pt-seq (SbfI), Splinkerette PCR, TeloPCR-seq
- DpnI (cuts only methylated/hemimethylated GATC; removes parental plasmid) [R]: Q5 SDM, QuikChange II, RF cloning
- Strand-displacing / non-displacing / proofreading polymerases and fidelity: Q5, Pfu/PfuUltra (no displacement), KOD, Phusion, KAPA HiFi (and Uracil+ variants that read through dU/DHU), Taq, AmpliTaq [U]. e.g. TAPS, redBS-seq, Q5 SDM, QuikChange II
- Klenow exo- (A-tailing, fill-in, dNTP-limited fill-in) vs Klenow exo+ [U]: Ribose-seq, RGEN-seq, SITE-Seq, scRRBS, scEdU-seq
- T4 DNA polymerase (3'->5' exo; 5'->3' fill; ligation-independent cloning overhang generation) [R]: SLIC, SMASH
- T4 PNK (5' phosphorylation, 3' phosphatase) [C]: SMASH, scEdU-seq, Q5/KLD
- T4 DNA ligase (sticky/blunt, concentration, PEG/crowding, overnight low-T) vs Taq DNA ligase (nick sealing, NAD+) vs SplintR/other [U]: RAD-seq, SAMOSA-Tag, RADD-seq
- Phi29 DNA polymerase: strand displacement, high processivity, MDA, RCA, 3'->5' exo (needs thiophosphate-protected primers) [C]: REPLI-g, TruePrime, snHi-C, RCA-GIP, SMM-seq, R-ChIP
- Bst large fragment (strand displacement, nick translation) [R]: RADD-seq, TrAEL-seq
- Polymerase I nick translation with DIG-ddNTP labelling [R]: SSB-Seq
- Tn5 transposase (and Tn5059, mosaic-end sequence, Tn5 loading with preannealed oligos, SDS stripping, gap fill 72C) [C-U]: s3-ATAC, sci-ATAC-seq family, scTAPS, scGET-seq, SPATAC-seq, Spatial-ATAC-seq

- Nucleases for chromatin: DNase I (hypersensitivity), MNase (nucleosome positioning, 3'-phosphate ends, Ca2+ dependent), S1 nuclease/mung bean (ssDNA-specific), ExoIII (3' resection), lambda exonuclease (5'->3', cuts 5'-phosphate dsDNA; RNA-primer protection), T5 exonuclease, Exo I/III/VII to remove linear DNA [C]: scDNase-seq, scMNase-seq, S1-seq, SNS-seq, TCC, smMIP

- Uracil excision: UDG + endonuclease VIII = USER; dU in adapters, cleaved hairpins/loops; Uracil+-tolerant polymerases; dUTP strand marking [C]: USER cloning, Pu-seq, TrAEL-seq, SMM-seq, RIMS-seq, S1-END-seq
- DNA repair glycosylases/AP endonucleases (hOGG1 for 8-oxoG, Endonuclease IV for AP sites) to convert lesions into gaps/ends [R]: RADD-seq, snAP-seq
- DNA methyltransferases as in-vivo/in-vitro probes: Dam (GATC m6A), M.CviPI (GpC 5mC), M.SssI (CpG), EcoGII (non-specific m6A, SAM cofactor, SAM replenishment) [R-C]: scDamID, SAMOSA, SAMOSA-Tag, SMAC-seq, SMAdd-seq

- Terminal transferase TdT (dA/dT/ddATP tailing; blocking ends with ddNTP; biotin-dUTP labelling; polyC tailing) [C]: RGEN-seq, ssDNA-Seq (permanganate), SSiNGLe, CATS
