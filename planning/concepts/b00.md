# Concepts, batch b00 (139 protocols: 10x scATAC ... GBS)
Basis: catalogue one-liners for all 139 + full-note reads/greps for novel ones (atrandi-wgs, ace-seq, esega, dm-seq, codec, botseqs, 6mace, fc-cet, 5hmC-SMRT, g4-seq, duplex-pro-seq). Frequency tags: [U]=ubiquitous (>30 protocols), [C]=common (8-30), [R]=rare/specialised (<8). Counts approximate. Protocol names shortened.

## 1. Enzymes: activities and specificities
- [U] High-fidelity proof-reading PCR polymerase (Q5/Phusion/KAPA): ~53 protocols; library PCR, amplicons. Proof-reading 3'->5' exo, error rate; chip-seq, atac-seq, artic.
- [C] Uracil-tolerant polymerase (KAPA HiFi Uracil+/Q5U): reads through U in template after deamination / UDG-containing templates. dm-seq, foodie, change-seq.
- [C] Klenow / Klenow exo-minus (~19): fill-in, 3' dA-tailing (exo-), nick translation-type extension. chip-seq, dnase-seq, 6mace-seq.
- [C] T4 DNA polymerase: 5'->3' fill-in plus 3'->5' exo chew-back for blunting. 6mace-seq, chip-seq, cap-seq.
- [C] T4 PNK: 5' phosphorylation and 3' phosphatase; needed to make ligatable 5'-P ends. chip-seq, dsb-seq, 6mace-seq.
- [C] T4 DNA ligase types (~12): blunt vs sticky vs TA ligation; PEG-enhanced; ligation needs 5'-P donor and 3'-OH acceptor. chip-seq, ddrad-seq, end-seq.
- [C] Tn5 transposase (~19): cut-and-paste tagging; loaded with adapter ends; fragmentation + adapter insertion in one step; 9 bp target-site duplication; stays bound to DNA (CPT-seq). atac-seq, cut-tag, chipmentation, change-seq.
- [C] Tn5 variants: pA-Tn5 / pA/G-Tn5 fusions (antibody tethered), uracil-bearing custom ends, "attenuated" Tn5 (partial digestion). cut-tag, cutac, change-seq, circulome-seq.
- [C] MNase (~8): Ca2+-dependent endo/exonuclease preferring linker/AT-rich; pA-MNase tethering; fused MNase (ChEC). cut-run, chec-seq, drop-scchip-seq.
- [C] phi29 DNA polymerase (~9): strand displacement, high processivity, 3'->5' proofreading; basis of MDA/RCA. atrandi-wgs, eccdna-circle-seq, dna-circle-sequencing.
- [R] Bst large fragment: strand-displacing, no exo; copy/primer extension. dm-seq, 6mace.
- [C] Thermostable Taq-type polymerases: no proofreading, add 3'-A; LongAmp for long amplicons. daf-seq, earth-microbiome.
- [C] Restriction endonucleases: Type II palindromic (HindIII, HaeIII, EcoRI, MboI/DpnII, NlaIII...) with defined overhangs; frequent vs rare cutters; methylation sensitivity (DpnI). 4c-seq, ddrad-seq, bl-hi-c, dnase-hi-c, gbs.
- [R] Type IIS/IIG cutters cutting away from site (MmeI, AlfI, BciVI, BsaI): cut tags of fixed length; 2b-RAD, DLO-Hi-C, drop-chip, combigem.
- [R] Homing/rare cutter PacI for adapter release. drop-chip.
- [C] Exonucleases: Exo III, lambda exo, ExoI, RecJ, Plasmid-safe: remove linear DNA to enrich circles; directional resection; T7 exo. eccdna-circle-seq, circle-seq-type, chip-exo, duplex-pro-seq, bl-hi-c.
- [C] USER (UDG + Endo VIII): excise uracil to open hairpin adapters / cleave deoxyU. end-seq, change-seq, five-six-letter, duplex-repair.
- [R] DNA glycosylases / AP endonucleases (UDG, PDG/FPG, APE1): remove lesions then nick, with UV-photolyase repair of CPD. circle-damage-seq, excision-seq, dna-circle-sequencing, duplex-repair.
- [R] Photolyase; T4 endonuclease V (CPD-specific nicking). circle-damage-seq, cpd-seq, damage-seq.
- [R] S1 nuclease: cuts ssDNA/flaps, converts overhangs to blunt. dsb-seq, circle-damage-seq, bless.
- [C] DNase I (DNase-seq, nuclear digestion, and removal of free/host DNA). dnase-seq, dnase-hi-c, atrandi-wgs.
- [C] RNase A / RNase H / RNase I / proteinase K: RNA removal, R-loop RNA:DNA hybrid digestion, protein removal after crosslink reversal. drip-seq, bisdrip-seq, chip-seq.
- [R] Terminal transferase (TdT): template-independent 3' tailing, biotin-dUTP labelling of DSB ends. dsb-seq, ace-type.
- [R] T7 RNA polymerase for in-vitro-transcription linear amplification (needs T7 promoter in adapter). bliss, chil-seq.
- [R] APOBEC3A/ A3A (deaminates C and 5mC, not 5-glucosylated hmC); DddB/ DddA-type cytidine deaminases on dsDNA. ace-seq, dm-seq, esega, foodie.
- [R] beta-glucosyltransferase (beta-GT): glucosylates 5hmC to protect it. ace-seq, dm-seq.
- [R] Hia5 (m6A methyltransferase) used as in-vivo/ in-situ footprinting probe. fiber-seq, dimelo-seq.
- [R] Lysostaphin, mutanolysin, lysozyme, achromopeptidase: lyse gram-positive cell wall for microbes. atrandi-wgs.
- [R] Cas9 in-vitro cleavage (RNP) as selective linearisation / enrichment / off-target discovery. change-seq, crispr-circle-seq, digenome-seq, discover-seq.
- [R] Recombinases/assembly enzymes: Gateway (att sites, BP/LR), Cre/lox, lambda Red/RecET recombineering, Gibson-type, CPEC overlap extension. gateway, et-recombineering, cpec, dna-assembler.
- [R] Debranching enzyme (resolve MDA hyperbranched structure). atrandi-wgs, cider-seq.
- [R] Single-strand-binding protein, uracil DNA glycosylase inhibitor etc. as helpers. atrandi-wgs/PTA.

## 2. Nucleic-acid chemistry and base conversions
- [C] Bisulfite conversion: C->U (deamination of unmethylated C; 5mC/5hmC protected); then PCR reads U as T; DNA damage/fragmentation; pre-ligation of methylated adapters. chip-bs-seq, drop-bs, dream, epigbs, bismapr.
- [R] Enzymatic conversion (TET/ glucosylation + APOBEC3A) as non-destructive alternative. ace-seq.
- [R] Chemical protection/labeling of oxidised cytosines: O-ethylhydroxylamine (5fC), cyclisation/ click enrichment (fC-CET), disulfide biotin labelling of 5hmC; reversible vs irreversible; 5caC. fcab-seq, fc-cet, 5hmc-chemical-label, dip-cab-seq.
- [R] Modified/nonstandard adapters: methylated or 5-propynyl-C (5pyC) adapters to survive conversion; distinguishing original from copy strand. dm-seq, epigbs, chip-bs-seq.
- [R] Nonstandard nucleotides (Z/P, dZTP, alpha-thio, ddNTP terminators). esega, atrandi-wgs/PTA.
- [R] Antibody enrichment of modified bases (5mC MeDIP, 6mA, BrdU/EdU incorporation, 5hmC). cfmedip-seq, 6mace-seq, e-l-repli-seq, chor-seq.
- [R] Nucleotide analogues for metabolic labelling (BrdU, EdU/click chemistry). chor-seq, d-nascent, e-l-repli-seq.
- [R] Lesion chemistry: CPD, adducts, oxidative lesions, DNA-protein crosslinks, G4 structures; polymerase stalling/ bypass. cpd-seq, damage-seq, adductseq, g4-seq, dpc-seq.
- [C] Formaldehyde / protein-DNA crosslinking and reversal; DPC enzyme trapping. chip-seq, chia-drop, dpc-seq-tg.
- [R] Click chemistry (CuAAC/azide) for labelling and enrichment. click-code-seq, fc-cet, chor-seq.
- [C] Biotin-streptavidin capture (biotin-dUTP, biotinylated hairpin/oligo, internal biotin-T); magnetic beads. bless, end-seq, bl-hi-c, chirp-seq.
- [C] Oligo end modifications: 5'-phosphate, 3'-blocking (ddC, amino-C6, 3'-spacer), phosphorothioate bonds (nuclease resistance), inosine, dU, internal biotin, 5' amino. atrandi-wgs, chip-nexus, crispr-ds.
- [C] Strand polarity and strand-specific libraries (dUTP, ligation-based orientation). duplex-sequencing, botseqs, drip-seq.
- [R] DNA structures as templates: G-quadruplex (cation/PDS-dependent stalling), R-loops (RNA:DNA hybrid, S9.6). g4-seq, drip-seq.
- [R] Hairpin adapters and loop/bridging design (Y-shaped, hairpin, forked, bridge). end-seq, codec, five-six-letter.

## 3. Oligo and adapter design features
- [U] Sequencing adapters P5/P7, Read 1/Read 2 primer sites (TruSeq/Nextera); adapter-dimers; 12 bp duplex core. atrandi-wgs, chip-seq, atac-seq.
- [U] Sample index (i5/i7), dual indexing, combinatorial indices, index hopping, Golay barcodes. 10x scATAC, earth-microbiome, ddrad-seq.
- [C] Fusion/ tailed primers: gene-specific region + adapter + barcode in one oligo. earth-microbiome, epicpcr, artic.
- [C] UMIs / molecular tags (random, within-adapter): PCR duplicate collapse; vs. natural endpoints as tags. bliss, chip-nexus, botseqs, crispr-umi, duplex-sequencing.
- [R] Duplex-specific complementary tags / duplex consensus; CODEC physical strand concatenation; two-strand family logic. duplex-sequencing, codec, duplex-repair.
- [C] Cell/droplet/bead barcodes; barcode diversity arithmetic (24^4); linkers/spacers. 10x scATAC, atrandi-wgs, dscatac-seq.
- [R] Cohesive overhang-defined barcodes/ ligation junction design; read geometry 8+4+8+4... atrandi-wgs, ch-atac-seq.
- [R] Template switching oligos / 5' cap / nanoCAGE concepts as source of mapping logic. 10x scATAC (cited concept).
- [R] Barcode-bead/PCR tail in droplets (three-block bead barcode). dscatac-seq, dsciatac-seq.
- [R] Hybridisation-based combinatorial barcoding. ch-atac-seq.
- [C] Restriction-overhang-compatible (sticky) adapters; unphosphorylated adapters producing nicked precursors; overhang-selective ligation. gbs, ddrad-seq, 2b-rad.
- [R] Inverted-T / forked adapters, NN-degenerate overhangs for 2b-RAD tag ligation. 2b-rad.
- [R] Blocking oligos / blocker DNA to prevent adapter or probe cross-hybridisation; blocking DNA in Hi-C. bl-hi-c.
- [R] Capture/tether oligos and splint/bridge probes (bridge capture, padlock/MIP, molecular inversion). bridge-capture, crispr-mip, digital-telomere.
- [R] Oligo-conjugated antibodies carrying barcodes or T7 promoters (DNA-barcoded antibodies, pMHC multimers). chil-seq, chip-dip, abseq, dna-barcoded-pmhc.
- [R] Bead-tag joining (shared bead barcode between separate DNA and antibody oligos). chip-dip.
- [R] Plasmid/guide barcoding: RSL, paired barcodes, guide-library design; multi-site mutagenesis variant libraries. crispr-umi, combigem, chymera, dimple.
- [R] Primer pools for tiled multiplex amplicons (alternating pools to avoid overlap interference). artic.
- [R] Degenerate/random hexamers incl. exonuclease-resistant (PTO) primers. atrandi-wgs, cider-seq.

## 4. Fragmentation
- [U] Sonication/ Covaris focused acoustic shearing; size distribution target. chip-seq, chip-bs-seq, bless.
- [C] Enzymatic fragmentation: fragmentase/FS (Ultra II FS), tagmentation, MNase, DNase I, restriction digest. atrandi-wgs, atac-seq, cut-run, dnase-seq.
- [R] Endpoint-defined cutting by Cas9 / nuclease site-specific cuts; coincident read starts. digenome-seq, discover-seq.
- [R] Bottlenecking dilution after ligation to create endpoint-defined families. botseqs.
- [R] Type IIS cutting to produce short tags. 2b-rad, dlo-hi-c.
- [R] Cryosectioning as physical fragmentation (GAM, 220 nm nuclear slices). gam.

## 5. End processing
- [U] End repair (T4 pol + Klenow + PNK), 5'-phosphorylation, blunting. chip-seq, drop-chip.
- [C] dA-tailing (exo- Klenow / Taq) for TA ligation; dATP-only tailing to avoid fill-in artefacts. duplex-repair, atrandi-wgs.
- [C] Fill-in of recessed ends (generate blunt or biotin-labelled ends). bl-hi-c, dlo-hi-c.
- [R] Dephosphorylation (CIP/rSAP) to prevent self-ligation/concatemers. dna-circle-sequencing, catch-seq.
- [R] Damage excision, limited fill-in, nick sealing before adapter ligation. duplex-repair.
- [R] Nick translation / nick sealing. epigbs, duplex-repair.
- [R] Circularisation (intramolecular ligation at dilution) and recircularisation. 4c-seq, dna-circle-sequencing, dnbseq.
- [R] Strand-displacing extension to link/ concatenate strands. codec, dm-seq.
- [R] Tn5 gap-fill (post-transposition 9 nt gap, 72 C extension). atac-seq, cut-tag.

## 6. Ligation types and logic
- [C] Blunt vs TA vs sticky-end ligation; ligation efficiency/ adapter excess; adapter-dimers. chip-seq, ddrad-seq.
- [R] Proximity ligation (Hi-C, 4C, DLO-HiC, Dip-C): crosslink -> digest -> fill-in/biotin -> ligate junction -> reverse, pull down biotin. 4c-seq, bl-hi-c, dnase-hi-c, dlo-hi-c, dip-c.
- [R] Half-linker/bridge linker design (MmeI cut of junction). dlo-hi-c, bl-hi-c.
- [R] Ligation-free proximity (barcode by droplet linear amplification; cosegregation). chia-drop, gam.
- [R] Split-pool ligation barcoding (multiple rounds, sequential). atrandi-wgs, ch-atac-seq.
- [R] On-resin / in-gel / in-plug ligation. dpc-seq-tg, end-seq.
- [C] Ligation on bead / in situ in nuclei. bliss, bless, chipmentation.
- [R] Single-stranded/ gap ligation; tether/ hairpin ligation for nanopore. digital-telomere, catch-nanopore.

## 7. Amplification modes
- [U] PCR for libraries: cycle number optimisation, qPCR to choose cycles, duplicates, bias, nested PCR. most protocols.
- [C] Inverse PCR with viewpoint primers. 4c-seq, chymera.
- [R] Linear amplification by IVT (T7) or primer extension: low-bias, no exponential error. bliss, chil-seq, chia-drop.
- [R] Rolling-circle amplification (RCA), RCA debranching; single-primer circles. eccdna-circle-seq, dna-circle-sequencing, dnbseq, cider-seq.
- [R] MDA / PTA / WGA (phi29, termination by ddNTP-alpha-S): amplicon bias, chimeras (hyperbranching). atrandi-wgs, daf-seq.
- [R] Emulsion/droplet PCR; single-cell PCR (SC-PCR); EpicPCR fusion of genes. duplex-pro-seq, epicpcr, drop-chip.
- [R] Multiplexed amplicon (tiled) PCR. artic.
- [R] Primer-extension + copy of original strand. dm-seq, fc-cet.
- [R] Tag-based cloning amplification (Gibson, CPEC, Golden Gate). cpec, dna-assembler.

## 8. Capture, enrichment, depletion
- [C] Immunoprecipitation (ChIP) with antibody + Protein A/G beads; input control; spike-in. chip-seq, chip-exo, chip-nexus, chipmentation.
- [C] Antibody-targeted in-situ cutting/ tagging (CUT&RUN, CUT&Tag, CUT&RUN.ChIP): pA-MNase/pA-Tn5, permeabilisation (digitonin), low-salt. cut-run, cut-tag, cutac.
- [C] Affinity capture (biotin-streptavidin, CXXC domain for unmethylated CpG, S9.6, antibody). cap-seq, dap-seq, chirp-seq.
- [C] RNA-templated capture: tiling biotin probes for RNA-chromatin (ChIRP). chirp-seq.
- [R] In-vitro TF binding libraries (DAP-seq, Bind-n-Seq): genomic DNA + tagged protein. dap-seq, bind-n-seq.
- [R] Hybrid capture/ probe capture; padlock/MIP; targeted capture of clone (CATCH). catch-seq, catch-cloning, capp-seq, bridge-capture.
- [R] Cas9-mediated enrichment (CATCH) for large fragments. catch-nanopore, catch-cloning.
- [R] Nuclease-based accessibility enrichment (DNase-seq, FAIRE phenol-chloroform). faire-seq, dnase-seq.
- [R] Restriction digest-based reduced representation (genome complexity reduction). 2b-rad, ddrad-seq, gbs.
- [R] Depletion of linear DNA/ host DNA; circle enrichment by exonuclease; topology-based gradient. eccdna-circle-seq, circulome-seq, atrandi-wgs.
- [R] Selection by cell sorting (early/late S fractions) and BrdU pulldown. e-l-repli-seq.
- [R] Carrier DNA to maintain recovery with low input. chip-bs-seq.
- [R] 5'-end or break-end specific labelling for DSB mapping (hairpin, biotin, adapter in situ). bless, bliss, end-seq, dsbcapture.

## 9. Cleanup and size selection

- [C] Gel extraction / PAGE size selection; agarose; Pippin. esega, 2b-rad.
- [C] Column cleanup; phenol-chloroform; ethanol precipitation with glycogen. faire-seq.
- [C] Strep bead washes and release (DTT, heat, NaOH, USER). end-seq, fc-cet.

## 13. Footprinting and native-DNA readouts
- [R] Footprinting with methyltransferase (Hia5 m6A), long-read, PCR-free native. fiber-seq, dimelo-seq.
- [R] Nanopore native DNA (BrdU signal; CATCH-nanopore; telomere). d-nascent, catch-nanopore, digital-telomere.

- [R] Sequencing as a probe of structure (G4, polymerase stalling; Na+/K+/PDS). g4-seq.

## 14. Sequencer-specific read structure / custom primers
- [U] Illumina flow-cell lawn P5/P7, bridge amplification, cluster, index reads, paired-end. all.
- [C] Custom sequencing primers (non-standard R1/R2/index read) and mixing primers; PhiX spike, denaturation NaOH for loading. earth-microbiome, duplex-pro-seq.
- [R] SOLiD, Ion Torrent, DNBSEQ (DNB RCA circularisation, patterned array). chip-exo, dna-barcoded-pmhc, dnbseq, ch-atac-seq.
- [R] Read-through/trimming of adapter sequences; read geometry mapping (barcode offsets, R2 with 8+4 etc.). atrandi-wgs.
- [R] Single-end vs paired-end; read length chosen by amplicon; overlapping reads. duplex-pro-seq.
- [R] Sequential multi-condition sequencing of same flowcell. g4-seq.
- [R] Low-diversity library handling for amplicons (PhiX). earth-microbiome.
- [R] 16S/ITS marker amplicons with Golay barcodes. earth-microbiome.

## 15. Higher-level logic / interpretation concepts
- [U] Duplicates: PCR vs optical vs natural; UMI dedup; library complexity.
- [C] Endpoint-resolved assays (exo-trimming, 5' end of read = cut site). chip-exo, chip-nexus, dsbcapture, end-seq.
- [C] Controls: input, IgG, spike-in, naked DNA, no-enzyme; synthetic controls.
- [R] Error correction: duplex consensus, single-molecule consensus (circle sequencing/rolling-circle consensus). duplex-sequencing, dna-circle-sequencing, codec.
- [R] Ancient DNA: partial UDG, terminal damage deamination (C->T), double-stranded library. double-stranded-ancient-dna.
- [R] Reference-distinguishing original vs synthetic copy. dm-seq.
- [R] Genotyping/reduced representation (RAD tags, restriction site density). ddrad-seq, gbs, 2b-rad.
- [R] Pooled variant/ barcode libraries and cloning (BioBrick, Gateway, Golden Gate, EMP cloning, DropSynth, DIMPLE saturation mutagenesis); barcode-to-variant mapping. biobrick, gateway, dropsynth, dimple, combigem.
- [R] Genome engineering assays: Calling cards (transposon), CAST-seq, CRISPR off-target (Digenome, CHANGE-seq, DISCOVER), crispr-ds. calling-card, cast-seq, change-seq.
- [R] Viral genome tiled amplicon sequencing. artic.
- [R] Repli-seq / replication timing (BrdU, S-phase sorting); origin mapping (bubble-seq). e-l-repli-seq, bubble-seq.
- [R] Chromatin accessibility (ATAC, DNase, FAIRE). atac-seq, faire-seq, fast-atac.
- [R] Protein/ cell identity via DNA barcoded antibodies (AbSeq, pMHC multimers). abseq, dna-barcoded-pmhc.

## 16. QC

- [C] Occupancy/ viability imaging (SYTO9 fluorescence); hemocytometer. atrandi-wgs.
- [C] Positive/negative control loci; spike-in genomes. cut-run, chip-seq.
- [R] Analytical aliquots vs production aliquots as QC practice. dm-seq.
