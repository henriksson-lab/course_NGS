# Batch b01 concept list (~134 protocols, genie-atac ... pta)

Frequency tags: [U] ubiquitous (>=15 protocols in batch), [C] common (5-14), [R] rare/specialised (2-4), [S] single-protocol specialist. Counts are rough.
Batch composition: chromatin-conformation (Hi-C family), Tn5/CUT&Tag family, methylation/modification mapping (bisulfite, enzymatic, chemical labeling), replication/DNA-damage/break mapping, cloning/assembly (toy examples, no NGS), CRISPR screen readouts, commercial kits (Illumina, NEB, ONT, PacBio), single-cell WGA, DNA-barcoded protein/glycan/peptide assays.

## 1. Enzymes: activities and specificities

- Type II restriction enzymes, 4-cutter (MboI/DpnII GATC, NlaIII CATG, HpaII/MspI CCGG, AluI, HaeIII, HpyCH4V TGCA, MseI): cut at defined palindromes giving defined (cohesive/blunt) ends; used to digest chromatin, fragment genomes, define fragment ends. [C] in-situ Hi-C, Hi-C original, PLAC-seq, Pore-C, NG Capture-C, NanoSeq, noir-seq, hyRAD, MSCC
- Restriction-site geometry: recognition site vs cut position, 5' vs 3' overhangs, top/bottom strand cut offsets; cut-site "scar" after fill-in/ligation (GATCGATC; HindIII->NheI). [C] Hi-C, in situ Hi-C, HiChIP, HiCoP, Methyl-HiC
- Methylation-sensitive/-dependent restriction enzymes (HpaII vs MspI; McrBC; FspEI/Mrr-family cutting methylated sites; MspI + M.SssI): read methylation as cut/no-cut or by tag counting. [R] MSCC, HELP-tagging, MethylRAD, Methyl-MAPS, MASD-seq
- Type IIS enzymes (BsaI, BsmBI/Esp3I, BpiI, SapI, EcoP15I, MmeI): cut outside recognition site at fixed offset; generate programmable overhangs or fixed-length tags (EcoP15I/MmeI = 20-27 nt genomic tags). [C] Golden Gate, GoldenBraid, MoClo, Loop assembly, lentiCRISPR/in4mer, HELP-tagging, MSCC
- Nicking endonucleases (Nb.BsmI, Nb.BsrDI, Nt.BbvCI): cut one strand only; create defined 3'OH for primer-free extension or strand-displacement amplification. [R] Nick-seq, PADSE-seq, NanoSeq-type, Nano-C(no)
- Homing endonuclease/rare cutter (I-SceI, SbfI, XhoI used for tag release/linearisation). [R] i-BLESS, hyRAD
- Tn5 / Tn5-like transposase: simultaneous fragmentation + adapter insertion (tagmentation); 9-nt staggered gap; needs 19-nt mosaic end (ME) duplex; produces gaps requiring 72C gap-fill. [U] Nextera XT, DNA Prep, Omni-ATAC, HiChIP, HiCAR, GenIE-ATAC, Mate pair, ONT rapid
- Loaded/engineered transposome variants: pA-Tn5 / pAG-Tn5 / nanobody-Tn5 (antibody-directed tagmentation), Tn5 loaded with single adapter (MEDS-A only), barcoded ME-A oligos, methylated adapters, photocleavable adapters. [C] Multi-CUT&Tag, MulTI-Tag, nano-CUT&Tag, NTT-seq, HiCuT, itChIP-seq, PSS, methyl-ATAC
- Tagmentation buffers/conditions: Mg2+ dependence (10 mM MgCl2 activates after antibody targeting), high-salt wash to prevent non-specific tagmentation, SDS/EDTA/neutralise to strip Tn5 off DNA. [C] CUT&Tag family, Nextera XT, Plate scATAC, Pi-ATAC
- Bead-linked transposome (BLT) and solid-phase tagmentation: tagmentation amount scales with bead-bound enzyme, so input-normalised fragment size; TSB stop + TWB wash. [R] Illumina DNA Prep (x2)
- Low-dose / dilute tagmentation for ultra-long DNA (MuA-like "fragmentation mix"); heat-kill at 75-80C. [R] ONT rapid, ONT ultra-long
- Tn5 transposome used for chromatin accessibility (ATAC): Tn5 inserts preferentially into open chromatin; nuclei prep (detergent mix, digitonin, Tween, mitochondria depletion). [C] Omni-ATAC, GenIE-ATAC, methyl-ATAC, Plate scATAC, Pi-ATAC, HyDrop-ATAC, HiCAR
- T4 DNA ligase (blunt/cohesive, ATP, PEG), E. coli DNA ligase (NAD+, nick-sealing, less blunt-end activity), Taq ligase (thermostable, nick/gap-fill ligation), AmpLigase, T4 RNA ligase (ss RNA/DNA ligation, PEG + Co(III) hexammine), CircLigase (ssDNA circularisation). [U] T4 ligase; [R] E. coli ligase (NOIR, HiDEF-seq, o2n-seq), RNA ligase (HydEn-seq), CircLigase (o2n-seq)
- Ligation enhancers/crowding (PEG8000, Ligation Enhancer) and temperature/time tradeoffs (dilute ligation to favour intramolecular, concentrated to favour intermolecular). [C] Hi-C family, NEBNext Ultra II
- DNA polymerases and exonuclease domains: Klenow (large fragment, 5'->3' pol, 3'->5' exo; exo-minus for A-tailing/fill-in), T4 DNA polymerase (3'->5' exo + 5'->3' pol: blunting/fill-in, overhang removal), Taq (non-proofreading, adds 3' A), Bst (strand-displacing, 5'->3'), phi29 (processive, strand-displacing, 3'->5' proofreading), T7 DNA pol, DNA pol I (nick translation), Q5/KAPA/Phusion (high-fidelity PCR), uracil-tolerant polymerases (KAPA HiFi Uracil+, Q5U, PfuTurbo Cx) for bisulfite/dU templates. [U]
- Terminal transferase TdT: template-independent tailing (dGTP/GTP etc.). [R] hyRAD
- Polynucleotide kinase T4 PNK (5'-OH -> 5'-P; 3'-phosphatase-minus variant to preserve 3' phosphate; 3'->OH conversion). [C] HydEn-seq, GLOE-seq, hyRAD, OK-seq, NSCR, Nick-seq
- Phosphatases (CIP, SAP, FastAP, Antarctic): dephosphorylate ends to block ligation (self-ligation, unwanted ligation) or remove 3'-P. [C] nCATS, Nick-seq, Micro-C, GLOE-seq(no), LCR/cloning, lentiCRISPR
- Exonucleases: ExoI (3'->5' ss), ExoIII (3'->5' ds), ExoVII, RecJf (5'->3' ss), lambda exo (5'->3' on 5'-P strand), T5 exo/5' exonuclease (Gibson): cleanup of primers, degrading non-circular/unprotected molecules, creating single-stranded overhangs. [C] ID-seq, MPX, Nick-seq, o2n-seq, Methyl-SNP-seq, MAESTRO, Gibson
- DNA repair enzymes: FFPE/DNA damage repair mix, USER (UDG + endo VIII) cleaves at dU, UDG/Endo IV/EndoVI, Fpg (8-oxoG excision), T4 endonuclease V (CPD), APE1 (AP site), RNase H (rNMP), T4 PDG. [R] ONT ligation, PacBio, USER adaptor NEBNext, GLOE-seq, Methyl-SNP-seq, o2n-seq, HiDEF-seq
- RNases/Proteinase K: RNase A (remove RNA), RNase If/RNase I, Proteinase K (reverse crosslinks, deproteinise, strip proteins before ligation); lyticase for yeast cell wall. [U] RNase A/PK; [R] lyticase (H4S47C)
- DpnI (cleaves Gm6ATC; removes dam-methylated parental plasmid after PCR). [R] IVA cloning
- Cas9 / Cas12a nucleases (RNP cutting in vitro): used for targeted cleavage of native DNA (nCATS), depletion of uninformative fragments (MC-4C, Nano-C), and as in-cell DSB inducer in the assay (GUIDE-seq, PEM-seq, LAM-HTGTS, INSPIIRED). [C] nCATS, MC-4C, Nano-C, GUIDE-seq, in4mer
- Nucleic-acid modifying methyltransferases: M.SssI (CpG), M.CviPI (GpC), SAM cofactor; used to protect/label (MAB-seq, MASD-seq, NOMe-seq, nanoNOMe, methyl-ATAC no). [R-C] MAB-seq, MASD-seq, NOMe-seq, nanoNOMe, MethylSaferSeqS(no)
- TET2 + T4-BGT (beta-glucosyltransferase) + APOBEC3A: enzymatic methyl-seq: TET oxidises 5mC/5hmC to 5caC/5gmC (protected), APOBEC deaminates unmodified C->U. [R] NEBNext EM-seq v2, Methyl-CODEC
- T4 BGT alone to transfer glucose (or azido-glucose) onto 5hmC for labeling/protection. [R] hmC-Seal, mTAG-seq(method), EM-seq
- Glycosylases/OGG1-type enzymes (context for lesion mapping), photolyase, Pol II inhibitors (NVP-2) as biological perturbations. [R] PADD-seq, OG-seq

## 2. Nucleic-acid chemistry & base conversions

- Bisulfite conversion: unmodified C -> U (reads T), 5mC/5hmC protected (read C); strand-destroying, strands no longer complementary, GC-poor imbalance -> PhiX spike. [C] MethylC-seq, PBAT, oxBS-seq, MAB-seq, Methyl-HiC, methyl-ATAC, LABS, Methyl-SNP, MethylSaferSeqS
- Ligation-first vs conversion-first (post-bisulfite adaptor tagging) WGBS: methylated adapters survive conversion (all C -> 5mC) vs adapters added after conversion by random priming (N4). [C] MethylC-seq, MAB-seq vs PBAT
- Methylated adapters/primers (5-methyl-dC in adapters, 5-methyl-dCTP fill-in) so only genomic C is converted. [R-C] MethylC-seq, methyl-ATAC, MAB-seq, LABS, MethylSaferSeqS, Methyl-SNP, Methyl-HiC
- Oxidative bisulfite (KRuO4 oxidises 5hmC->5fC): subtractive BS vs oxBS to get 5hmC. [S] oxBS-seq
- Enzymatic conversion (APOBEC) and chemical-free methylation readout: base-resolution without DNA damage. [R] EM-seq v2, Methyl-CODEC
- Enrichment-based methylation (MBD2/MeCP2 pull-down; salt-gradient elution): methylated DNA binding, no base conversion. [R] MiGS, MethylCap-seq
- Methylation-dependent cut-counting/tag methods: MspI/HpaII differential digestion, adapter + MmeI tag, T7 amplification. [R] MSCC, HELP-tagging, MethylRAD, Methyl-MAPS (also methyl-seq Brunner)
- Methylase footprinting (accessibility via exogenous GpC methylation; HCG/GCH/WCG context rules; GCG ambiguity). [R] NOMe-seq, nanoNOMe
- 5hmC chemical labeling (T4-BGT azido-glucose + click-biotin) and selective chemical labeling of other marks: KAS-seq (N3-kethoxal labels ssDNA guanine), OG-seq (iridate oxidation traps 8-oxoG), NAIL-seq/OK-seq/MINCE-seq (EdU/BrdU metabolic labels), Ini-seq (DIG-dUTP), GLOE/HydEn (end chemistry). [C] hmC-Seal, mTAG-seq, KAS-seq, OG-seq, MINCE-seq, NAIL-seq, OK-seq, Ini-seq
- Click chemistry: CuAAC (Cu(I), THPTA, ascorbate) vs copper-free SPAAC (DBCO/azide, TCO/tetrazine); used for biotinylation of nucleotides, antibody-DNA conjugation, glycan-DNA, DTT-cleavable disulfide linkers. [C] KAS-seq, MINCE-seq, NAIL-seq, OK-seq, hmC-Seal, mTAG-seq, ID-seq, NGGM, LIGA, PADSE-seq
- Bioconjugation chemistries: NHS ester, SMCC/thiol, maleimide, thiol-oligo, azide/DBCO; carrier reagents. [C] ID-seq, ProteinSeq, PBA, PADSE-seq, LIGA, NGGM, NSCR
- Biotin-streptavidin capture (biotin-dNTP fill-in, biotinylated primers/adapters/probes, internal biotin-dT, dual biotin, desthiobiotin; MyOne C1 vs T1 beads; high-salt, urea, SDS washes; alkaline/NaOH denaturation to release non-biotinylated strand). [U] Hi-C family, GLOE-seq, GUIDE-seq, OG-seq, LAM-HTGTS, PEM-seq
- Digoxigenin / antibody-based capture of labeled DNA (anti-DIG, anti-BrdU). [R] Ini-seq, NAIL-seq
- Modified oligo end chemistries: 5'-phosphate (ligation competent), 3'-amino / 3'-C3 spacer / 3'-ddC / 3'-phosphate (blocked extension/ligation), 5'-amine, phosphorothioate bonds (nuclease resistance, protects adapter ends), 2'-O-methyl RNA, dU internal (USER-cleavable), LNA/BNA (blockers), 8-oxoG/dU in flow-cell primers, photocleavable linker, hexaethylene glycol spacer. [U]
- Phosphorothioate nucleotides: alpha-thio-dNTP (nuclease-resistant copy, Nick-seq NT) and alpha-thio-ddNTP (irreversibly terminating phi29; PTA). [R] Nick-seq, PTA
- Dideoxy / chain terminators for end blocking (ddNTP + Pol I, ddBTP A-tailing blocking) so pre-existing ends can't be extended or A-tailed. [R] Nick-seq, NanoSeq, HiDEF-seq, GLOE (no)
- Nucleic-acid end states: 5'-P/5'-OH, 3'-OH/3'-P, blunt, 3'-dA overhang (TA ligation), 5' overhang (fill-in), 3' dT overhang on adapter, cohesive-end ligation; phosphatase/kinase manipulates them. [U]
- Single-strand vs double-strand substrates: ssDNA library prep (CircLigase, ssDNA adapter kit, bridge adapters with random overhangs, splints) vs dsDNA libraries; alkaline denaturation. [C] OK-seq, NAIL-seq, OG-seq, HydEn-seq, o2n-seq, GLOE-seq, NSCR
- Hydroxide hydrolysis (KOH) to expose ribonucleotide sites as 5'-OH ends; RNA-DNA chimeric strands; RNaseH2 / alkaline cleavage. [S] HydEn-seq; [R] GLOE-seq, NSCR
- Uracil excision chemistry (dU in primer/adapter + UDG/USER) as a strand-selective cleavage switch; dUTP second-strand marking. [C] NEBNext USER adaptor, o2n-seq, Methyl-SNP-seq, MethylSaferSeqS, MAESTRO, PBA
- Metal-catalysed DNA cleavage (Cu-phenanthroline hydroxyl radical on histone-tethered label): sequence-independent nucleosome-dyad mapping. [S] H4S47C chemical mapping
- UV photo-chemistry (254 nm CPD formation, 365 nm Hoechst/BrdU strand nicking, photocleavable linkers, photocaged adapters + ROI illumination). [R] PADD-seq, NanoStrand-seq, PSS
- Formaldehyde crosslinking (1-3%, glycine quench), DSG/protein-protein crosslinkers, dual crosslinking; reverse crosslink with Proteinase K at 65C. [U] all Hi-C/ChIP derivatives
- Protein-DNA affinity chemistry: ConA bead immobilisation of nuclei/cells, digitonin permeabilisation, protein A/G bead capture, His-tag pulldown, Dynabeads; antibody-primary/secondary sandwiches. [C] CUT&Tag family, MapR, HiChIP, MINT-ChIP
- Hybridisation / annealing physics: Tm, GC content, degenerate/IUPAC bases, SSC/Denhardt/Cot-1 blocking, stringency washes. [R-C] hyRAD, MAESTRO, NG Capture-C, flow-cell capture OS-seq

## 3. Oligo / adapter design features

- Illumina adapter architecture: P5/P7 flow-cell binding arms, Read1/Read2 sequencing-primer sites, i5/i7 index slots; TruSeq-type vs Nextera-type (Tn5 mosaic end) layouts; adapter "Y/forked" structure from partial duplex. [U]
- Y-adapters / forked adapters (non-complementary arms): prevent adapter-dimers and enforce asymmetric ends; "half-functional adapter" needing PCR to complete. [C] GUIDE-seq, Hi-C original, NEBNext, Methyl-ATAC, GLOE-seq, PADD-seq
- Hairpin / stem-loop adapters (single oligo with U loop opened by USER; closed SMRTbell dumbbell; 5'-blocked stem-loop in ThruPLEX): self-ligating-resistant structures. [R] NEBNext USER, SMRTbell (x2), HiDEF-seq, ThruPLEX (i-BLESS), MASD-seq hairpin
- Bridge / linker oligos (internally biotinylated bridge between two ligated fragments, chimeric junction marker). [R] Long-read ChIA-PET, LAM-HTGTS (bridge adapter), PEM-seq, NAIL-seq, hiCAR
- Splint oligos (bridging ligation templates, random-hexamer splint, cognate splint ligation): hybridisation-templated ligation. [C] GLOE-seq, HiCAR, ProteinSeq, PSS, HydEn-seq (random-primed 2nd strand)
- Sample (multiplexing) indexes: single vs dual (combinatorial), unique dual indexes (UDI), index hopping avoidance, index length 6/8/10, inline barcodes vs separate index reads, Golay barcodes (error-correcting). [U]
- UMIs / molecular identifiers: random N-mer on adapter or primer (8-15 nt) to collapse PCR duplicates; duplex UMIs (both strands) for duplex consensus; UMI vs sample barcode vs random splint degeneracy distinction. [C] GUIDE-seq, MASD-seq, MAESTRO, MethylSaferSeqS, NanoSeq (random), NOIR, PBA, MPX, ID-seq, NEBNext UMI-PCR-free
- Random priming degeneracy (N4/N6 priming foot) vs UMI: priming sites are not identifiers. [C] PBAT, MALBAC, PicoPLEX, GLOE-seq, HydEn-seq
- Staggered / phased primers (0-3 N stagger) to diversify base composition on low-diversity amplicon libraries; PhiX spike-in. [R-C] Joung one-PCR, nano-CUT&Tag, NTT-seq, HiCAR, 16S, in4mer
- Modality / target barcodes in barcoded transposomes (S5-barcode-connector-ME), antibody-coded transposomes to multiplex histone marks in one tube. [R] Multi-CUT&Tag, MulTI-Tag, NTT-seq, nano-CUT&Tag
- Tailed (two-stage) PCR design: locus-specific 3' part + universal 5' overhang; PCR1 enrichment then PCR2 index/adapter addition; nested primers; one-PCR vs two-step. [C] 16S, Joung, Moffat, lentiCRISPR, ID-seq, inverse PCR, ProteinSeq, PEM-seq, GenIE-ATAC
- Primer degenerate bases / IUPAC codes (V3-V4 16S). [R] 16S, NOIR
- Linear amplification / single-primer extension (LAM, PEM biotin primer, linear PCR) vs exponential PCR: avoids PCR bias/ jackpotting; keeps strand identity. [R] LAM-HTGTS, PEM-seq, GenIE-ATAC, NOIR, NanoStrand-seq, MethylSaferSeqS
- Bait / probe oligos: biotinylated capture probes, T7-promoter-bearing viewpoint probes (Nano-C), allele-specific probes (MAESTRO), flow-cell-surface capture primers (OS-seq), telomere-overhang baits with phase arms (telobait). [C] NG Capture-C, MAESTRO, Nano-C, OS-seq, telobait, hyRAD
- Oligo pools / array synthesis with amplification handles and cloning into vectors; guide arrays with direct repeats (ordered, multi-guide). [R] lentiCRISPR v1/v2, in4mer, Moffat, MoClo family
- Standard "enzymatic" custom oligo types: 5' amine for conjugation, thiol-C6, azide, DBCO; AOC (antibody-oligonucleotide conjugates) with protein-ID barcode + UMI + pixel ID. [R] MPX, PBA, ID-seq, ProteinSeq, PADSE-seq
- Padlock probes & circularisation: oligo with ligatable ends hybridising adjacent to target, closed by ligase, then RCA. [R] MPX, PBA (RCA pixels)
- Telomere / defined-end baiting: G-rich overhang annealing + EcoRI release. [S] PacBio telobait
- Carrier DNA/ssDNA carriers to protect low-input material; spike-in controls (lambda unmethylated, pUC19 methylated, ERCC-like), mock samples, no-enzyme controls. [C] EM-seq, MAB-seq, oxBS, methyl-ATAC, META-CS, Methyl-SNP

## 4. Fragmentation / DNA size definition

- Acoustic shearing (Covaris) vs sonication (Bioruptor/Branson; cycles, duty) vs enzymatic (FS fragmentase, restriction, Tn5, MNase, DNase I) vs hydrodynamic (g-TUBE, needle) vs chemical (copper hydroxyl radicals, base hydrolysis). [U]
- Target size / insert-size choice: 150 bp (cfDNA-like), 300-600 bp (Illumina), 10-30 kb (HiFi), >50 kb N50 (ultra-long); relation to read length/cluster efficiency. [U]
- MNase digestion: ssDNA/dsDNA exo-endonuclease; Ca2+ dependent; nucleosome-protected ~147 bp; titration to mono-/di-nucleosomes; MNase-fused proteins (MapR RHdelta-MNase). [R-C] Micro-C, MINT-ChIP, ORGANIC, MapR, H4S47C(no)
- DNase I with Mn2+/Ca2+ (IPOD-HR) and MPE-Fe cleavage (MPE-seq): sequence-dependent vs independent footprinting. [R] IPOD-HR, MPE-seq
- Fragment-size effect of enzymatic fragmentation (FS) and combined fragmentation/end-prep/A-tailing one-tube reactions. [R] NEBNext Ultra II FS
- Fragmentation before vs after the key modification step (GLOE-seq captures native ends before shearing; BLESS/i-BLESS label ends before shearing; Hi-C shears after ligation; HiChIP shears after ligation). [C] design principle
- Mechanical protection of long DNA: agarose plugs / agarose beads for DSB or HMW work; avoiding bead-based cleanup (precipitation) for ultra-long; wide-bore tips. [R] GLOE-seq, i-BLESS, ONT ultra-long
- Short-read fragmentation-free amplification of long molecules (PTA proceeds without fragmentation). [S] PTA

## 5. End processing

- End repair (T4 DNA pol + Klenow + PNK): blunt + 5'-P; combined with A-tailing (Klenow exo-minus / Taq dATP 3'A) for TA ligation; ONT/NEB Ultra II "End Prep" combined. [U]
- A-tailing & TA-ligation vs blunt ligation (A/T overhang increases specificity and prevents concatemers). [U]
- Dephosphorylation to prevent adapter self-ligation or to select for fresh ends (nCATS, GLOE-seq, Micro-C, Nick-seq). [C]
- End blocking/capping then selective unmasking (dephosphorylate -> Cas9 cut -> new 5'-P ligation targets); 3'-block with ddNTP. [R] nCATS, Nick-seq, GLOE-seq
- Fill-in of restriction overhangs (Klenow with biotin-dNTP, or plain dATP in HiCuT); blunt-end proximity ligation; loss/gain of restriction site at junction (diagnostic). [C] Hi-C family
- Gap repair / strand displacement of Tn5 9-nt gaps (72C extension, or T4 DNA pol / strand displacement enzyme). [C] Tn5 methods, Mate pair, HiCAR
- Overhang removal / single-strand trimming (DNA Prep Enzyme in SMRTbell). [R] PacBio express
- DNA damage repair (FFPE repair, USER, glycosylase+AP endonuclease). [R] ONT, PacBio, HiDEF-seq
- Dilute (intramolecular) circularisation and exonuclease cleanup of linear products (mate pair circularisation; 4C/inverse PCR circularisation). [R] Nextera mate pair, inverse PCR, HiCAR, MC-4C

## 6. Ligation types

- Sticky/cohesive-end ligation (restriction-defined overhangs; Golden Gate overhangs; adapter with T overhang). [U]
- Blunt-end ligation (proximity ligation in Hi-C; adapter to blunt Tn5 ends). [C]
- TA-ligation to dA-tailed fragment; dT-tailed native-barcode adapter (ONT). [U]
- Intramolecular (circularising) vs intermolecular ligation; dilute vs concentrated; in-nucleus (in situ) ligation vs in solution. [C] Hi-C vs in situ Hi-C, HiCAR, Mate pair
- Proximity ligation: ligation of crosslink-proximal DNA ends in fixed nuclei yields chimeras; random vs spurious (cross-sample) ligation; trans vs cis; concatemers (Pore-C, MC-4C). [C ~18] Hi-C family
- Splint-mediated ligation, bridge-adapter ligation (ssDNA to ssDNA via random hexamer overhang), hairpin-adapter ligation, ligase-mediated gap fill. [R-C]
- Ligation of motor-protein adapters (ONT LA/NA/RAP): sequencing adapter carries motor + tether; cohesive attachment. [R] ONT x4
- Cloning-style assembly: Type IIS one-pot digestion/ligation (Golden Gate, MoClo, GoldenBraid, Loop), isothermal overlap (Gibson, In-Fusion, IVA, MEGAWHOP, PIPE), ligase cycling (LCR), ligation-independent cloning (LIC-PCR, T4 pol 3'->5' exo with single dNTP), inverse PCR. [R] mechanisms; they end in constructed DNA, no NGS
- Antibody-DNA proximity ligation (ProteinSeq: two probes + splint), gap-fill ligation of RCA pixel IDs. [R]

## 7. Amplification modes

- PCR (cycles, hot-start, high-fidelity vs processive, annealing/extension choice, 2-step vs 3-step, limited cycles for index PCR, qPCR cycle-number determination, over-amplification/duplicates, chimeras). [U]
- Nested / semi-nested PCR; two-round PCR with different primer sets; inverse PCR; linear (single-primer) amplification; asymmetric PCR; suppression PCR (primer-annealing blocks panhandle). [C-R]
- PCR bias: GC content, size bias, UMI deduplication, polymerase choice for uracil templates, PCR-free workflows (SMRTbell, ONT, ultra II FS PCR-free, PBAT) and PCR-free vs low-input tradeoff. [C]
- Linear amplification in vitro transcription (T7 IVT): promoter-bearing adapters; RNA-based linear amplification and re-RT; carrier selection (MINT-ChIP, HELP-tagging, LIANTI, Nano-C, MC-4C guide production, LABS). [R] MINT-ChIP, HELP-tagging, LIANTI, Nano-C, LABS
- Reverse transcription of IVT product (RT with random hexamer + handle) back to cDNA. [R] MINT-ChIP, HELP-tagging, LABS
- Rolling circle amplification (phi29 RCA of padlock/circle; concatemeric ssDNA "DNA pixels"); uracil-containing RCA. [R] MPX, PBA
- Multiple displacement amplification (phi29 + random exonuclease-resistant hexamers; branching hyperbranched products; chimeras; GC bias). [R] Microbe-seq, PTA (variant)
- Primary template-directed amplification (PTA): phi29 + terminators to limit product length -> quasi-linear, uniform; less chimeric. [S] PTA
- Quasi-linear WGA: MALBAC (looping, 27-nt common handle, 8-nt random foot, Bst 8 cycles, then PCR), PicoPLEX (quasi-random primers, 12 linear cycles, then PCR), DOP-PCR. [R] MALBAC, PicoPLEX
- Transposon-based linear amplification (LIANTI: T7-promoter transposon + IVT; META-CS: tag-based; Tn5-tagged strands via carrier). [R] LIANTI, META-CS
- Strand-displacement amplification with nicking enzymes (SDA) for DNA scanning. [S] PADSE-seq
- Primer extension with Taq/Pol on circularised templates (Methyl-CODEC: Phi29 strand-displacement linkage of duplex strands). [R] Methyl-CODEC
- On-flow-cell amplification: bridge amplification, flow-cell primer engineering (cleavable C/D primers: 2-dU/8-oxoG), target-specific surface capture (OS-seq). [S] OS-seq
- Emulsion / in-droplet barcoding PCR (Microbe-seq: MDA, tagmentation, barcoding PCR on beads). [S] Microbe-seq
- Plasmid-library amplification (oligo pool PCR with ArrayF/R). [R]

## 8. Capture / enrichment / depletion

- Streptavidin enrichment of biotinylated junctions or labeled strands (see biotin). [U]
- Hybridisation capture: in-solution biotinylated probes (xGen, Twist), double capture, hot washes, blocking oligos/Cot-1, probe-from-library baits (hyRAD from MseI/SbfI-digested RAD fragments, BioNick). [R-C] hyRAD, MAESTRO, NG Capture-C, Methyl-SNP (exome), Nano-C
- Allele-selective capture (stepdown hybridisation with mismatch discrimination). [S] MAESTRO
- Antibody immunoprecipitation (ChIP: protein A/G, stringent washes; targeted tagmentation CUT&Tag; CUT&RUN/MapR MNase release). [C] HiChIP, PLAC-seq, HiCuT, long-read ChIA-PET, MINT-ChIP, ORGANIC, Multi-CUT&Tag family, MapR, PADD-seq, IPOD-HR
- Sequential/serial selection (IP then lesion selection; ChIP then biotin pulldown). [R] PADD-seq, HiChIP, PLAC-seq
- Protein-free DNA enrichment/depletion by phase partitioning or silica columns (phenol interface; FAIRE-like; HiCoP). [R] IPOD-HR, HiCoP
- RNA-guided Cas9 depletion of uninformative fragments (negative enrichment). [R] MC-4C
- Methyl-binding domain capture / MBD (MiGS, MethylCap). [R]
